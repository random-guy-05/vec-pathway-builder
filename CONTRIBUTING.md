# Contributing

Corrections and useful route suggestions are welcome.

- Keep official facts linked to a current official Challenge page; avoid duplicating volatile limits, dates, and metric weights in the app.
- Keep routes short, task-aware, and explicit about the limits of any recommendation.
- Do not label a community contribution as accepted unless the official page marks it accepted.
- For language edits, preserve the English intent, the distinction between official sources and community resources, and the warning that the app does not score or upload files. Native-speaker review is especially useful for biological and competition terminology.
- The app should remain usable without a server, analytics, dependencies, or access to participant files.
